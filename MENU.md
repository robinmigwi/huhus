4. **Coffee:** Whether Flat White is available as a double.
5. **Healthy blends:** Whether Hydrant Shot uses apple, pineapple or either.
6. **Flame-grilled chicken / grill menu:** No separate flame-grilled chicken menu or grill prices were visible in the supplied images. This category is not invented here and needs another source from Huhu's.
